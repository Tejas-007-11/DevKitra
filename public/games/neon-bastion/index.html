How to Play NEON BASTION

🎯 Aim & Fire: Move your mouse or drag anywhere to aim. Hold left click or Spacebar to fire continuous energy projectiles.

🛡️ Defend the Core: Geometric enemies spawn from the arena perimeter and charge your central bastion. Destroy them before they inflict damage.

🔷 Enemy Types:
• Drifter (Cyan): Direct steady approach.
• Sprinter (Yellow): High-speed arrow, fragile.
• Tank (Purple): Armored hexagon with high health.
• Zigzag (Green): Sinusoidal evasive maneuvers.
• Splitter (Orange): Bifurcates into smaller micro-shards on death.
• Shielded (Blue): Frontal deflector shield.
• Bomber (Crimson): Detonates in a lethal blast near the core.

⚡ Upgrades & Bosses: Clear waves to unlock 8 stackable upgrades (Rapid Fire, Multi Shot, Explosive, Orbital Shield, etc.) and conquer geometric bosses!
