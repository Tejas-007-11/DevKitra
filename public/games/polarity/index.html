How to Play POLARITY

🧲 ATTRACT (Cyan): Magnetic nodes pull your metal orb toward them. Use this to accelerate into turns and slingshot around obstacles.

⚡ REPEL (Magenta/Orange): Magnetic nodes push your orb away. Use this for emergency braking, leaping gaps, and bouncing off walls.

⭐ Energy Orbs: Collect floating energy orbs for extra stars and to unlock gated pathways.

🌀 Exit Portal: Navigate past hazards, laser gates, and moving drones to safely dock inside the emerald vortex.
